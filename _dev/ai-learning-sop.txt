================================================================================
COMPLIANCE MAKER — HOW THE AI DECIDES, AND HOW IT LEARNS
Standard operating procedure, in plain English.
================================================================================

Two things are described here:

  PART ONE — the path a single clause takes to become an answer.
  PART TWO — how a corrected answer changes what happens next time.

One thing to be clear about before either: THE MODEL ITSELF NEVER CHANGES.
Its weights are fixed. What improves is what it is TOLD each time — the facts,
the past answers, and the section descriptions supplied with the clause. This
is memory and retrieval, not training. The practical effect is the same (it
gets better the more you use it) but the mechanism matters when you are trying
to work out why an answer was wrong.


================================================================================
PART ONE — THE PATH TO AN ANSWER
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 0 — BEFORE ANY AI IS INVOLVED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Most rows never reach the model at all.

  1. The PDF is parsed in the browser. Clauses, section headings and part
     headers are separated, and each clause is given its position in the
     hierarchy (for example: PART 2 PRODUCTS -> 2.02 CASING).

  2. Highlighting is applied — standards, materials, numbers, ALL-CAPS terms.
     Cosmetic only. It never affects an answer.

  3. LIBRARY PRE-FILL (signed-in users). Every clause is compared against the
     product/factory library workbook.
       - Exact text match  -> filled, marked "from library" (yellow)
       - Close match       -> filled, marked "from library" (yellow)
     A filled row is REMOVED from the AI's work. The model is never asked
     about a clause the library already answers.

  4. CONFLICT CHECK. Each filled answer is compared with the most recent
     answer logged for that same clause. A disagreement is flagged red with
     the previous answer shown. Nothing is overwritten — you are told, and
     you decide.

  Only rows still blank after all of this go to the model.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 1 — WHAT TRAVELS WITH EACH CLAUSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Clauses are sent in batches of three. Each one carries:

  - the clause text
  - its position in the specification hierarchy
  - up to 5 past verified answers to similar clauses
  - the learned profile of its section, if one exists
  - (shared by the batch) the datasheet fields, if a datasheet was uploaded
  - (shared by the batch) the reference facts judged relevant to these clauses

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 2 — THE LADDER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The model works down and stops at the first step that resolves the clause.

  STEP 1  DATASHEET      Project-specific. Beats everything.      verified: TRUE
  STEP 2  LIBRARY        What was answered before.                verified: TRUE
  STEP 3  CONSTRUCTED    Pattern-following, no invented facts.    verified: FALSE

Step 3 may only produce Comply or TO VERIFY. Deviation and Not Comply require
a real source, because guessing either one misrepresents the factory just as
badly as a false Comply does.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 3 — WHAT COMES BACK, AND HOW IT IS MARKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  ORANGE   verified: true    Backed by datasheet or library.
  AMBER    verified: false   A guess. The Compliance cell also gets a
                             literal "[GUESS]" prefix, so the marking
                             survives even if the colours are stripped or
                             the Comments column is deleted.
  RED                        Contradicts a previously logged answer.
  YELLOW                     Came from the library, never reached the model.

Two independent markings for a guess — colour and text — because the export
gets edited, recoloured and forwarded, and one of the two usually survives.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 4 — WHEN THE MODEL RETURNS NOTHING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

An empty response is treated as retryable, not as a verdict:

  Pass 1   full prompt
  Pass 2   same clauses, stripped-down prompt   (length is the usual cause)
  Pass 3   one clause at a time                 (nearly always lands)

Only after all three does a row come back unanswered, and it is then left
blank for a human rather than filled with something invented.


================================================================================
PART TWO — HOW IT LEARNS
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE ONE RULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  IT NEVER LEARNS FROM ITS OWN UNCONFIRMED OUTPUT.

An AI-sourced answer that nobody has checked is excluded from every rollup and
every profile. This is the single most important line in the whole design.
Without it the tool would treat its own guesses as evidence, grow more
confident with every run, and drift steadily wrong while looking increasingly
authoritative. That failure is very hard to notice from the inside, because
everything continues to look consistent.

Learning requires a human decision. There are no exceptions to this.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE LOOP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. AI PROPOSES.
     Every suggestion is recorded immediately — clause, section, status,
     remark, and whether it claimed to be verified.

     WHY IMMEDIATELY: the exported file only ever carries the FINAL text. If
     the suggestion is not stored when it is made, a corrected answer becomes
     indistinguishable from an answer nobody touched, and the whole comparison
     is impossible.

  2. A HUMAN FINISHES THE MATRIX.
     Offline, in Excel, the way the work is done anyway. No new habit.

  3. SOMEONE WITH TRAINING ACCESS UPLOADS IT.
     "Library & answer log" -> Upload completed matrix.

  4. EVERY ROW IS COMPARED AND CLASSIFIED. (The correction matrix, below.)

  5. EVERY CONFIRMED ROW ENTERS THE ANSWER LOG.
     Marked source = "confirmed". From that moment it drives library pre-fill,
     the conflict check and the section profiles FOR EVERY SIGNED-IN USER.

  6. SECTION PROFILES REBUILD AUTOMATICALLY.
     Answer counts and typical statuses update on upload. Summaries written by
     an admin are LOCKED and are never overwritten.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE CORRECTION MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Each uploaded row is compared with what the AI proposed for that same clause.
The "[GUESS]" prefix is stripped before comparing — shipping that text
untouched means the answer was accepted, not rewritten.

+------------+--------------------------------+------------------------------+
| VERDICT    | WHAT IT MEANS                  | WHAT HAPPENS                 |
+------------+--------------------------------+------------------------------+
| ACCEPTED   | Shipped exactly as suggested.  | Recorded as confirmation.    |
|            | The AI was right.              | Answer enters the log as     |
|            |                                | confirmed and pre-fills for  |
|            |                                | everyone.                    |
+------------+--------------------------------+------------------------------+
| CORRECTED  | Shipped differently. A human   | The HUMAN answer wins, and   |
|            | overruled the AI.              | permanently. Both versions   |
|            |                                | are kept side by side so the |
|            |                                | disagreement is visible.     |
+------------+--------------------------------+------------------------------+
| NEW        | No suggestion on file. Either  | Treated as a confirmed human |
|            | the library answered it, or it | answer. Enters the log,       |
|            | predates AI review, or nobody  | pre-fills, feeds profiles.   |
|            | ran AI on it.                  |                              |
+------------+--------------------------------+------------------------------+

An ACCEPTED answer and a CORRECTED answer both enter the log as confirmed.
Being right and being fixed are equally good outcomes — both produce an answer
a human stands behind, which is the only thing the log is for.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
READING THE CORRECTIONS — WHERE THE REAL IMPROVEMENT COMES FROM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

/admin/ -> Compliance shows the running tally and the last 40 corrections.
The full history downloads as Excel from Library & answer log -> Corrections
log, with the AI's version and the shipped version side by side.

This is the most useful document the system produces. Read it looking for
PATTERNS, not individual mistakes:

  THE SAME CORRECTION, REPEATEDLY
    A missing or wrong FACT. Fix it once in /admin/ -> Compliance -> Reference
    facts and the whole class of error stops. This is the highest-value repair
    available and it takes about a minute.

  ONE SECTION CORRECTED FAR MORE THAN OTHERS
    The section profile is wrong or missing. Edit the summary by hand; your
    edit locks and will not be overwritten.

  AMBER (GUESS) ROWS CORRECTED CONSTANTLY
    Step 3 is guessing badly for this product. Either the reference facts are
    too thin to pattern-match from, or the library is too small yet. More
    confirmed answers is the fix; there is no shortcut.

  ORANGE (VERIFIED) ROWS BEING CORRECTED
    The most serious signal in the system. The AI claimed a real source and
    was still wrong, which means either a datasheet field was misread or a
    library answer is itself wrong. Investigate these first, every time —
    a wrong library answer will keep propagating until it is corrected at
    source.

  BY CONTRACTOR ROWS BEING CHANGED
    The site-execution keyword list disagrees with how your team draws the
    scope line. That list is currently in code, not in the database — worth
    raising if it happens often.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHO CAN DO WHAT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  SIGNED OUT
    Convert, highlight, preview, download Excel. 10 pages.
    Nothing pre-fills. No database is touched at all.

  SIGNED IN
    50 pages. Library pre-fill, conflict checks, answer logging.

  + AI CLAUSE REVIEW (granted per user)
    Datasheet upload and AI answering.

  + TRAINING (granted per user, SEPARATELY)
    Upload completed matrices. Download the answer log, the library file and
    the corrections log.

  WHY TRAINING IS A SEPARATE GRANT: running AI on your own work affects your
  document. Uploading a completed matrix rewrites what EVERY other user is
  shown from then on. Those are different levels of trust and should be
  granted separately, to different people if you want.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HONEST LIMITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Clause matching is word-overlap, not meaning. Two clauses saying the same
    thing in different words will not match. Embeddings would fix this and are
    the single biggest available quality improvement.

  - The model is small and fast, chosen for cost and latency. A larger model
    would follow the ladder more reliably, at more cost per matrix.

  - Nothing verifies that an uploaded matrix was actually checked. The
    training grant is the only safeguard, and it is a human safeguard. Give it
    to people whose work you would sign.

  - Corrections apply from the moment they are uploaded. Matrices already
    downloaded do not update.

  - A wrong answer confirmed by upload becomes a wrong answer for everyone,
    with no automatic way to notice. The corrections log is the place it would
    show up, as the same clause being corrected back and forth.
