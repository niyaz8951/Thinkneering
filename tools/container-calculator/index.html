How the numbers are worked out

  • Enter packed sizes — crate, skid and protection included, not the bare equipment.
  • Vehicle figures are internal usable dimensions. Door openings are smaller than the box; check the tallest item against the door height before you book.
  • The packer places large items first and fills from the nose backwards. It is a fast heuristic, not a proof of the theoretical minimum — treat the answer as a solid working estimate to confirm with your forwarder.
  • Centre of gravity is reported as a percentage of the vehicle length from the nose. Aim for 45–55%; outside that band, axle loads and lashing need a second look.
  • Nothing leaves your browser. Files you upload are read locally and never sent anywhere.
