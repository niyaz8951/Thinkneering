Tips

Detected labels include section codes (1.01, 1.02), letters (A., B.), numbered items (1., 2.) and part headers (PART 1). A scanned PDF has no text to extract — this tool does not use OCR. Review the preview before exporting; complex or multi-column layouts may need a quick tidy-up.
