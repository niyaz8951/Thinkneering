## 2. What changed and why

### Compliance Maker

**Heading.** `Compliance Maker` is now the `h1`. "Specification to compliance
matrix" sits under it as a lede line (`.page-head__lede`, added to
`global.css`), so the page is titled by the product and described by the task.

**Product and Factory alignment.** The real cause was
`.field + .field { margin-top: var(--space-3) }` — a rule for stacked fields
that also fired inside `.field-row`, pushing every field after the first down
by exactly one spacing unit. Fixed in `global.css` with
`.field-row > .field + .field { margin-top: 0 }` plus
`align-items: flex-start`, so the inputs stay level even when one label wraps
and the other does not. This fixes every side-by-side field pair on the site,
not just this one.

**The chat bot.** This was not a prompt problem. `ask.js` returned
`{ answer: "<prose string>" }`, while the front end read `d.answer.status` and
`d.answer.remarks` — treating the string as an object. Both came back
`undefined`, so it fell through to the literal default `'TO VERIFY'` and
rendered nothing else. The natural-language answer was arriving and being
discarded on every question.

The contract is now `{ status, remarks, answer }`:

- `answer` — two to four sentences, the spoken reply
- `status` — one of *Comply*, *Comply with remarks*, *Not Applicable*,
  *Noted*, *TO VERIFY* (no "Deviation", no "Not Comply")
- `remarks` — the sentence that would go in the Remarks column

The reply is rendered as the body, the status as a coloured chip beneath it,
and the remark only when it says something the answer does not — so you get
the natural tone with the matrix judgement attached, rather than one or the
other. Status parsing tolerates case and near-miss wording, and a model that
returns prose instead of JSON has its prose kept rather than thrown away.

I also removed a short-circuit I had added against `matchCriterion`: those
rows are value-comparison rules with a `status` of `'trusted'`, not canned
answers, so treating them as answers would have been wrong.

### Education

Three hops became one. `/s/education` → the Humonks card → straight into the
book at the chapter you last read.

- The Canon Bible was a separate book only because it came from a second
  `.docx`. It is now chapters of `bk_humonks`, prefixed `Canon —`,
  `sort_order + 100` so they always sort after the narrative however many
  chapters it gains. They keep `access_level = 'restricted'`, so **merging did
  not widen access** — they stay locked without a grant.
- `sec_edu_humonks` is deleted and the book item moved up to `sec_education`.
  NCERT keeps its sub-section: it is a real grouping that will hold several
  books; Humonks held exactly one.
- `/s/education/humonks` still resolves, for old links and bookmarks.
- `/read/humonks` no longer shows a "Start reading / Continue reading"
  landing page. It resolves where you were and `location.replace()`s there —
  `replace`, not assign, so Back returns to the section rather than bouncing
  through the resolver again.

Reading position is now stored twice on purpose: `localStorage` is instant and
works signed out, and the new `reading_progress` table follows the account to
another browser or device. Whichever was written more recently wins. The
server write is debounced to one per two seconds of settled reading, with a
final write on `pagehide` so closing a tab mid-chapter still records the spot.
If the remembered chapter has since been unpublished or locked, resume falls
back to the first chapter you *can* open rather than stranding you on a
paywall.

### Single session

`login.js` inserted a new session row without removing the old ones, so every
sign-in simply stacked another valid session. Nothing enforced the limit.

Now the insert is preceded by
`DELETE FROM sessions WHERE user_id = ?`, plus a sweep of rows past their
expiry so the table does not grow unbounded for people who never sign out.
`currentUser()` already returns null when the session row is missing, so the
previous device is signed out cleanly with no further change.

The comment above it asks the next person not to optimise it away. That is
deliberate — it looks like a redundant delete before an insert, which is
exactly how it got lost.

## 3. Things I found while checking the merge

You asked whether everything was streamlined. Three things were not.

**The knowledge tools were orphaned.** `/tools/knowledge/` and
`/tools/process-map/` existed and answered on their URLs, but no section or
item in the catalog pointed at them — they appeared in no nav and on no
section page, reachable only by typing the address. `2026-08-catalog-knowledge.sql`
registers a **Knowledge** section holding both, marked `restricted` /
`member`, which matches how their APIs already behave.

**Nothing gated them at page level.** The APIs enforce auth and per-map
access, so your data was never exposed — but a signed-out visitor got a
working-looking shell where every button 401s. `_middleware.js` now redirects
those two paths to `/login/?next=…`. Compliance Maker is deliberately left
out: it has a genuine free tier that runs in the browser.

**All four knowledge and process-map pages were missing the Google Fonts
link** every other page carries, so they rendered in system fonts rather than
Sora/Inter/JetBrains Mono. Added.

Also: asset cache-busting had drifted — most pages were on `?v=4` while the
merged-in pages were on `?v=12`. Everything is now `?v=5` for
`global.css`/`global.js`, and the Compliance Maker scripts moved to `?v=2`.

### Removed from the zip

- `.git/` — 350-odd loose objects; your local repo is the source of truth.
- `.wrangler/cache/wrangler-account.json` — **contains your Cloudflare account
  ID.** Worth checking whether it is committed in your repo history:
  `git log --all --oneline -- .wrangler/` — and adding `.wrangler/` to
  `.gitignore` if it is not there.
- `_dev/` — a byte-identical duplicate of three files already in
  `docs/compliance-maker/`.

## 4. Smoke test after deploying

1. **Compliance Maker** — heading reads "Compliance Maker"; Product and
   Factory sit on one line with their inputs level. Pick AHU + UAE, open
   **Ask**, ask *"can we comply with 62mm panel?"* You should get a sentence
   or three, with a coloured status chip under it — not a bare `TO VERIFY`.
2. **Education** — `/s/education` shows the HUMONKS card and NCERT. Clicking
   HUMONKS lands directly in a chapter. Read a little, leave, come back: it
   resumes. The contents list shows the Canon sections after Chapter Three,
   locked unless you have the grant.
3. **Sessions** — sign in on your laptop, then on your phone. Reload the
   laptop: it should be signed out.
4. **Knowledge** — signed out, `/tools/knowledge/` should bounce to the login
   page. Signed in, it should appear under Knowledge in the nav.

## 5. Still open

Not touched in this pass, and worth knowing:

- **The knowledge → Compliance Maker bridge is not wired.** `/api/knowledge/search`
  exists and works, but Compliance Maker does not call it yet. The staged plan
  is in `docs/compliance-maker-integration.md`; stage one is shadow mode —
  call it alongside the spreadsheet, change no answers, and log what it cannot
  answer. Do that before cutting anything over.
- **Single session has no "signed in elsewhere" message.** The old device just
  finds itself logged out. A clearer notice needs a tombstone row or a reason
  code on the session table; say the word and I will add it.
- **The knowledge seed is still mostly unapproved drafts**, so it answers
  nothing downstream until someone reviews it. That is the intended gate, not
  a bug.
