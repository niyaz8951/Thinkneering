# Regional Project Development Engineer — Executive Summary

**HVAC Solution Consultant · TQRQ/DSRQ Process Owner · Booking Enablement Lead · Business Process Automation**

---

## In one paragraph

I own the regional technical enquiry pipeline for a multinational HVAC business, from the
moment a consultant specification lands to the point an order is booked. The role sits at
the join between what a specification demands and what a factory can economically build —
Sales, Application Engineering, Product Engineering, Controls and Global SBU on one side,
five manufacturing sites across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Europe, India and China on the
other. I convert technical requests into commercially viable solutions, and I have rebuilt
the process that carries them so the conversion is fast enough to matter.

---

## Pipeline ownership

I run the TQRQ and DSRQ pipelines across MEA markets end to end: intake, technical review,
factory clarification, special-option pricing, proposal, booking, closure. Ownership here
means more than routing. It means knowing which enquiries are worth escalating, where a
pipeline is ageing and why, and which stalls are structural rather than one-off.

The failure this role exists to prevent is the quiet one: an enquiry that is complete on
our side, waiting on someone who does not know they are the blocker, ageing out of
relevance while everyone assumes it is being handled.

## Technical depth

I review consultant specifications against what can actually be supplied — deviations,
compliance strategy, special options, certifications, controls and BMS integration, ATEX
scope, acoustics, hygienic construction and filtration — across AHUs, FCUs, chillers, ERVs
and custom equipment.

The judgement that matters is not whether we comply. It is deciding what to comply with,
what to deviate on, and how to argue the deviation so it survives consultant review. A
technically perfect offer that loses on price has not solved the problem; neither has a
cheap one that fails the specification.

## Booking enablement

I support conversion through value engineering, alternative product proposals, compliance
strategy and optimised factory pricing. The work is commercial as much as technical:
identifying where a specification leaves room, obtaining the special quotation that makes
a bid competitive, and reducing the technical risk that makes a deal fragile.

## Cross-functional coordination

I am the interface that keeps five factories, six internal functions and the consultant
speaking the same language. Each has a different definition of "done", a different lead
time and a different tolerance for ambiguity. Coordinating them is a large part of the
job, and doing it without adding a layer of delay is most of the skill.

## Automation and productivity

I build the tooling that removes the manual load from this process: Excel VBA costing and
selection tools, Power Automate routing and assignment flows, enquiry-tracking dashboards,
KPI monitoring and management reporting.

The reason for building it is not efficiency for its own sake. Manual tracking fails
silently — nobody notices the enquiry nobody picked up. Automated tracking makes the
status of every enquiry visible without anyone having to ask, which changes what gets
escalated and when.

### A note on using this

This summary is written from a role description, not from the record. Every claim in it
is a claim about capability rather than a measured result.

The SBU map exists to close that gap. Every enquiry you work through it leaves a closed
action with an outcome — what was asked, who answered, how long it took, what it cost.
After a quarter of that, the numbers in a summary like this stop being assertions and
start being things you can point at: actual response times by stage, actual conversion on
enquiries you touched, actual factory turnaround by site. That version of this document
will be worth considerably more than this one.
